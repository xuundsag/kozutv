# V39 — Hindi availability and verification (partial, not deployed)

When Vidwave Hindi cannot be reached, the Japanese/English choices are shown (when present on MegaPlay), not an unverified Hindi choice or other regional choices. When Hindi is verified, the available regional choices can also be shown. Hindi discovery runs asynchronously and verifies the source response; failure can also be transient and does not prove that a dub does not exist. A direct or next-episode Hindi source-resolution failure falls back to MegaPlay Japanese with a notice. MegaPlay remains the provider for Japanese/English. If a catalogue record lacks its AniList ID, the server looks it up using its MAL ID before probing Vidwave; this fixes discovery for titles such as Jujutsu Kaisen and Demon Slayer. When an AniList lookup or Vidwave is down, Hindi may be hidden until retried.

Real local browser and signed-proxy playback tests for Vidwave's Hindi-labelled episode 1: Alya Sometimes Hides Her Feelings in Russian (AniList 162804, MAL 54744); Naruto (20); Solo Leveling (151807); Jujutsu Kaisen (113415); Demon Slayer (101922); Attack on Titan (16498); Death Note (1535). Each advanced past three seconds with media ready. Alya's player was also sought to 120s and decoded audio and video, and its episode 2, episode navigation and reload were verified. Audio language was not independently transcribed or listened to in the automated environment; 'Hindi' is the provider's designation. Audio-menu test selected Hindi for Alya and Jujutsu Kaisen and switched to Vidwave. One Piece episode 1, which returned an inaccessible Vidwave source, showed only Japanese and English; selecting its unavailable Hindi URL fell back to Japanese. Report database submission was retested and the test record deleted.

Known limitations: One Piece and Naruto Shippuden's sampled Vidwave links returned NestZone 403 in this environment; official backup returned 400 main-only. Probe success does not guarantee complete playback or language accuracy, nor does failure prove a dub does not exist. Titles/episodes/controls were not exhaustively verified. Caption issue and V35 watchlist ordering/background persistence remain unresolved. EmbedWave is removed as a selectable server but remains in internal fallback/audio/subtitle code. Vidwave does not currently provide a verified movie/TV replacement. This archive includes the real .env and must stay private. Service worker version kozutv-v39. Not deployed.
