# Episode metadata and artwork sources

The project combines episode information from multiple independent sources. Provider numbering and broadcast orders can differ. The data is selectively matched and edited, not an authoritative or complete episode guide.

- `perman-titles.json`: English titles were compiled from [ČSFD's Superkid episode list](https://www.csfd.cz/en/film/517490-superkid/episodes/) and, for selected entries, the [Perman Wiki list of episodes](https://perman.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Perman_episodes). The Fandom-derived entries are adapted, selectively matched and corrected; the [Perman Wiki contributors](https://perman.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Perman_episodes?action=history) make community text available under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). That license applies to the relevant Fandom-derived text, not automatically to the software or to third-party artwork.
- `shinchan-regional-titles.json`: a reference list of early broadcast segment names adapted from [FZ Cartoons' Shin-chan episode list](https://fzcartoons.wordpress.com/sin-chan/). Only individually checked title matches are assigned to regional-provider episode numbers. Some provider entries repeat or use a different order.
- `doraemon-s1-regional.json` and the `doraemon-s1-*` stills: names and stills from the [Internet Archive season-one item](https://archive.org/details/doraemon-s1-hindi-with-episode-name); all 52 runtimes matched the regional provider and selected content was spot-checked. Later season titles fall back when no reliable match is available.
- `shinchan-*` stills were sampled from the corresponding available regional-provider videos. If a matching still is missing, the app uses show artwork or a neutral placeholder. Stills may reflect the particular source's edit or watermark.
- Other episode metadata and images are sourced at runtime from services including AniList, Ani.zip, TheTVDB, Jikan/MyAnimeList and TMDB, subject to those services' terms and availability. V5.4.13's higher-resolution anime Spotlight portraits are matched TMDB poster images, requested at runtime, with the previously bundled local art as fallback.

Anime characters, frames, show artwork and other third-party content remain the property of their respective rights holders. The above credits are not a claim that the project owns that content or may relicense it.
