# V42 — partial movie/TV audio and quality

- Restored EmbedWave for movie and TV server selection, fallback and regional audio enrichment of other providers. Anime Hindi remains on Vidwave; the old EmbedWave anime regional code remains disabled. Captions are out of scope and the old EmbedWave caption resolver was not restored.
- Select among playable regional-audio candidates using HLS-master measured maximum resolution first, then a source-label quality hint when the manifest provides no resolution. H.264 remains preferred. Labels and resolution are not guarantees of image clarity or bitrate, and a single-variant stream cannot be upscaled into better detail. Unknown dimensions are no longer marked 1080p internally.
- Actual browser checks: Avengers: Endgame, EmbedWave, Hindi-labelled option selected; its HLS offered 480/720/1080 and manual 1080 was enabled and decoded at 1080 video height while playback progressed. Stranger Things S1E1 via VidCore with EmbedWave regional enrichment selected Hindi-labelled source and decoded at 1080 height while playback progressed. Language identification relies on provider labels, not independent listening. Availability changes and some titles have fewer tracks/qualities.
- Tests: JS syntax, test-v5413d, test-genre-cache, and two real Playwright playback checks. Existing runtime matching is heuristic and cannot prove a dub is the correct cut or episode; source labels may be inaccurate. Stream probing inherits the previous provider implementation's network/SSRF risks and should be hardened before exposing to untrusted inputs.
